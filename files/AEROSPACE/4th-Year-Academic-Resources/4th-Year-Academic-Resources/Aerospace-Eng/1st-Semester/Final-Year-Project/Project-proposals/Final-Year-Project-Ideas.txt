Opoku's project description
Solar circuit with step up voltage ability

The project aims to extract solar power and step it up to power home AC devices.

It has come to my realization that whenever there is power out, most people find their homes uncomfortable due to heat, poor ventilation, and other devices(freezers,cookers etc) seize operation causing lots of problems.
As as result, the project seeks to build a standby power supply circuit that will automatically be operate when there is power out to save work and increase comfortability.

The project will focus on building a circuit that combines a step up transformer with a transistorized inverter.
The circuit will be powered by solar cells and will have voltage regulators as well. 
The solar cells will store enough energy during the day and will be used when necessary to power the circuit, the inverter will provide AC power as required while the voltage is step up by the transformer.
Note: The main goal is to build a simple circuit which will do the job
