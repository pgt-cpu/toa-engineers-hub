Note that, to denote the distribution factors, a symbol (DXX) is employed here which is slightly different from that (DFXX) used in precise moment distribution. This is because the present distribution factors represent directly the ratio of the stiffness of the member concerned to the sum of the stiffnesses of all the members meeting at that joint, and are thus the same as the distribution factors used in normal moment distribution. They therefore differ from the modified distribution factors employed in precise moment distribution, which are derived from the restraint factors.
