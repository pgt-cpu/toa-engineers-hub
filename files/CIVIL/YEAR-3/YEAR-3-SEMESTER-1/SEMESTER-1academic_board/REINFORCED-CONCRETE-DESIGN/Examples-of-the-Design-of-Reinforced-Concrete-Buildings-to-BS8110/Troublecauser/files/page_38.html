As with the design methods in previous use, the moment of resistance of a section reinforced in tension and compression is found by adding the resistance resulting from the couple due to the concrete in compression and its balancing tension steel, to the couple due to the compression reinforcement and additional tension steel. The first part of the analysis is therefore identical to the case where only tension reinforcement is provided.
