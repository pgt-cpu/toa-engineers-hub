For partial uniform and triangular loads, values of K1Ms can be read from the charts on Data Sheets 34 and 35, and values of Ms, the free moment on span AB, can be read from Data Sheets 26 to 28. As before, virtually any loading may be considered by subdividing the load into

combinations of partial loads, and summing the values of K1Ms and of Ms (multiplied by their respective loads) for the individual loads concerned. The value of K1 appropriate to the total load considered is then obtained by dividing ΣK1Ms by Ms max, where Ms max is the maximum free moment on the span and 1/rs is the curvature at the position at which this moment occurs.

When θ has been calculated, the resulting deflection at the tip of the cantilever is θlc, where lc is the span of the cantilever. If θ is positive in the above formula this deflection will be upwards, and vice versa.
