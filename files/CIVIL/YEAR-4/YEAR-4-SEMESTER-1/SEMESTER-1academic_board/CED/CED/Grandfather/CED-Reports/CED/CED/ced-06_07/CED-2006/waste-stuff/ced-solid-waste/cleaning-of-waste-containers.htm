INTRODUCTION:

Studio WTB develops tailor made industrial cleaning systems. Used as barrel washers (click for information), crate washers, bin washers and high end industrial systems for reactor cleaning. Light applications can often be handled by standard machinery. Many cases however require dedicated approach.

WASTE MANAGEMENT 

Waste management culture is on the move. You can read more on the Dutch Government policy on a sub page of this site: click here  the article "Dirty Business" as published in a Dutch magazine (An English summary will be provided) For more info about working conditions and hygienic aspects click here

A waste processing company has set up a logistic system for collecting different types of waste. Typical products are industrial kitchen waste, so called "swill" and glass bottles. The waste is collected by several clients in wheeled waste bins of different sizes, from 120 to 260 liters. A waste bin equipped with a lockable cover (see picture above) is of common use for the swill waste. 

Bin collecting trucks gather filled waste bins and transport them to a central processing location. By the same truck clean, empty bins are left behind. On the work floor first the full bins are emptied in large steel containers using hydraulic lifting & tilting devices.  Every day a constant flow of emptied waste bins is lined up on the work floor. Are they dirty?....see the pircture!

The client only accepts cleaned bins. Initial method is cleaning manually using high pressure guns. This soon turns out to be a dirty, heavy and stultifying job. Productivity turns out to be low.

 APPROACH:

Studio WTB analyzed this special, high volume application.  Examination of working conditions, combined with experience of earlier projects, learned that the system had to be fully automated. A handling system without the need for adjustment between different sizes of bins during work should be necessary. Buffering facilities should offer the operator the required flexibility - and would spare 1 operator. No lifting by the operator is considered to be acceptable at a capacity of at least 470 bins per day.

Less visible but major important: environmental + hygienic aspects & costs per washing. The system has to offer precisely the required cleaning power resulting in a minimum consumption of fresh water, detergent and energy. The production floor offered a surplus of space, at first view,  nevertheless a compact system has to be designed for keeping enough possibility for shovel and collecting truck traffic 

The Studio WTB investigation effected in a new machine concept. This concept is described, the estimated "cost of ownership" is calculated and the turn key proposal is presented to the waste collecting company.

LAY-OUT  

 

1 - THE HIGH PRESSURE HOT WATER INSTALLATION

Not only the cleaning machine requires hot water, also a separate facility for washing large containers and other equipment manually is supplied by the hot water system. 

Studio WTB provided the total washing system.

As shown on the picture a platform offers better organization on the work floor. Studio WTB designed and supplied a practicable platform on which the pump is placed. 

The Dual pump unit has two stationary pumps. Both pumps receive their water from one combined boiler. Heating energy is generated by a natural gas burner. The capacity of the heating system covers both high pressure pumps:  60oC water and a total of 37 ltr/min (9.7 US gallons/min)  On both places spraying can take place simultaneously. 

Underneath the platform 2 pcs. of 1.000 ltr. (265 US gallon) bins contain cleaning detergents. Different types of soap can be used for the bin cleaning machine and manual cleaning.  About the usage of soap in the bin washing machine an interesting sub - page offers information, click here.  

2 - THE POSITION- AND TRANSPORT SYSTEM

The principle of operation of the system is based on an overhead conveyor. Main part of this a system, mainly used in abattoirs and coating installations, is a steel profile. Inside this profile a chain in running. This chain can bend in all directions so the system can follow any track. The chain moves due to a drive station which is part of the track powered by an electric motor. 16 pcs. steel brackets with roll-on platforms are attached to the chain on special lugs.

A strong advantage of this system is having the technical parts literary on a high level. Water as well as dirt fall downwards.....Due to this lay-out the transporting system remains more dry and clean. 

An overhead conveyor offers additional safety against injuries. Furthermore this type of conveyor prevents the floor around the machine for becoming a jungle of all kind of conveyor supports. Man does not have to climb over the conveyor track to reach the inner circle of the system. A similar system is used in another project of Studio WTB, the barrel washer.  Photo: Critical examination prototype by persons involved.

3 - THE WASH CABIN

The wash cabin is manufactured totally of stainless steel. In the lower part of the cabin a water reservoir is integrated. The contents is 3.000 liter (800 US gallons) The water surface is covered by 6 pcs sieve plates. These plates, on which the remains are gathered, can be taken easily out of the machine. 

The cabin has a special designed closing system. The closing system offers protection against vapors and spattering. The door construction evaluated a simple, rugged construction.    

For inside cleaning two Clean Operators®  are mounted: The first works at 4 bar (60-80 psi) and with double solid spraying beams. Electrical power rate of the pump 5,5 kW
The second works with high pressure 125 bar (1800psi) and hot water 60oC and has a separate dosing system for cleaning detergents. Electrical power rate of the pump is approx 7 kW

Both spraying heads can be stitched inside the bins, nearly reaching the bottom. The movement of the head is carried out by Clean Extenders® - type XL.  Studio WTB has developed a complete range of these positioning devices. The extenders are air driven.

PROCESS, working with the machine:

Operator personnel tilts the waste bin slightly and then rolls the bin easily onto the platform. There is no need for lifting the bin. The total model range of the client can be handled in the machine randomly. There is no need to adjust anything to platform or machine between sizes: just a matter of extensive designing & prototype testing. The cover is opened by the operator and placed between bars. It remains in position during the total cycle - cannot close or flap over.

The machine makes cycles. The transport system runs a certain time to transport one stitch. During transport pumps are shut off: Cabin doors are opened now.

The machine has 16 platforms. This may be a larger number. Once the machine is started it continues automatically. The only action the operator has to do is changing the bins, working on the outer ring of the machine. No button pressing required.The machine gives optical and acoustic warning signals before the conveyor starts. Just in front of the cabin the platform is being tilted. Large drippy remains can be lost before the cabin. On the picture the large outlet of the water basin  is visible.

First washing stage: low pressure 4 bar (60 psi) Picture shows two custom rotating spraying heads - for effective exterior washing of the bin. These rotating heads are driven by water pressure.  For easy assembling are the heads are mounted on a quick release connection.

The Clean Operator Heads follow their own movement pattern, programmed for this application. High- and low pressure head patterns are different. 

The heads move as follows:

  1. stitch in,  rotate,  beams poined towards the bottom 
  2. rotate - stitched in - spraying beams slowly moving upwards 
  3. retract head, spraying towards inside surface of cover 
  4. continue rotating, spraying beams to upper edge of the bin 

The second Clean Operator, high pressure, features a dosing system for detergents.

The characteristic advantage of the controlled spraying head is it's ability to be pointed exactly towards the spot that needs cleaning. It is possible to freeze the head some time in a certain position. Studio WTB started developing this device in 1994 en patented the machine internationally.  This device has it's own page on this internet site with interesting info, animations and pictures, click here.

After washing the container is transported outside the cabin still tilted. Water remains drip off. During the following cycle the bin returns upright. Drying continues and - depending on the conditions inside the production building -  the bin is almost dry when it returns in front of the system. 

ECONOMY

The capacity of this machine is determined by a surplus of parameters. This lay-out of the machine can handle 30 to 120 bins per hour. Some parameters are: Level of contamination of the container, temperature and pressure of the spraying water, type of detergent and number of revs per minute of the spraying head. 

The described machine operates at a capacity of 62 bins per our (approx 500 per day - 8 our shift) and takes - in this situation - work over from 4 persons. The pay back time of the installation can be estimated at 1,5 years. About economy of the machine a separate page is available. CONCLUSION:

The installation offers benefits: Cleaning goes fast,  low cost (compared to manual washing) and safely.  The environment gets less saddled up with detergent remains. The programmable Clean Operator heads prevent spillage of water & detergent - they only spray where to clean.
