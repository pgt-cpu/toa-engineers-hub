According to the European definition, a lysimeter is a vessel containing local soil placed with its top flush with the ground surface for the study of several phases of the hydrological cycle, e.g. infiltration, runoff, evapotranspiration, soluble constituents removed in drainage, etc.

Therefore, the term lysimeter does not include suction cups.
