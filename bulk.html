Bulk add resources

Pick your organised folder and this page makes all the labels in one go. Best on a laptop or computer.

Step 1: Choose your folder

Pick the main folder that holds your files, for example one that contains Programme, Year, Semester and Course folders. Nothing is uploaded here. The page only reads the names.

Step 2: Check the list

Yellow rows are guesses and red rows are missing something. Fix them using the boxes. Untick any file you do not want.

Step 3: Make the labels

How to finish

  1. On GitHub, open your project, then the files folder. Tap Add file, then Upload files, and drag your whole main folder in. Wait for it to finish and tap Commit changes.
  2. Open resources.json, tap the pencil, put your cursor at the end of the line "resources": [, press Enter, and paste the labels. Tap Commit changes.
  3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then check the Resources page.

GitHub's website takes about 100 files at a time and up to 25 MB per file. For bigger batches use the free GitHub Desktop app: drag the folder into your project folder, then Commit and Push.
